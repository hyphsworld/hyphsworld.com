HYPHSWORLD // BUILD YOUR WORLD

Your Creator World walkthrough.

New here? Start below. Already approved? Use CREATE and My Creations to keep building.

How to go from application to Live

  1. Apply to build your World.

    Sign in with your HYPHSWORLD ID, then apply for Creator World. Add your creator or brand name, creative categories, city / region, contact email, portfolio or public work link, and creator story (at least 40 characters). Creator World isn’t open enrollment. Every application gets human review.

  2. Check your application and unlock your tools.

    Return to this dashboard to check your application message. Pending or In review means wait for a decision. Needs info means use Update Application, add the requested information, and send it back. Not approved means read the review notes before applying again.

    Once approved, select Build My Creator World if it appears. If you already have a World, your dashboard tools appear here. Application approval unlocks your private World; it does not publish your profile or work.

  3. Set up your World.

    Use Profile to edit your creator details and choose a new profile picture from your device. Check the preview, then select Save Profile and wait for confirmation. Your categories help match the Creator Loadout on Overview. Check Trust & Tools for your available tools and verification status. Badge review is separate from application and creation review. If you own more than one World, choose the right one in MY CREATOR WORLDS before creating.

  4. CREATE your first piece.

    Select ＋ CREATE. Choose Music, Video, Artwork, Merch, or Creator World update. Enter a title, choose a file, then press CREATE. Use MP3, WAV, or M4A for music; MP4 or MOV for video; JPG, PNG, or WEBP for artwork; images or PDF for merch. A World update can use these supported files.

    50 MB maximum per file. If a file is too large, export a smaller version first. Wait for the saved confirmation. CREATE saves your upload privately; it does not make it public.

  5. Manage your work and send it for review.

    Use the saved creation card or My Creations. Filter by type, Open Preview to check the file, and Rename to update its title while it is editable. When it is ready, select Send for Review and confirm. It stays private during review.

    In review? Make Private takes it out of the review queue. Changes needed? Read the Owner note, make the requested changes, then send it for review again. To submit a corrected file, CREATE a new version with a clear title. Approved and Live creations do not show Rename or Send for Review.

  6. Know when it is approved and Live.

    Check each creation’s status badge in My Creations. Reload this dashboard to check for a new review decision.

    Private
    Saved in your World; not submitted for review.
    In review
    Sent to HYPHSWORLD for review; still private.
    Changes needed
    Read the Owner note and prepare the requested update.
    Approved
    Review passed; still waiting for publication.
    Live
    HYPHSWORLD completed publication. Public display also requires your Creator World profile to be published.

    Only authorized HYPHSWORLD reviewers approve work and use PUBLISH TO WORLD. You do not need to find a publish button in your dashboard. Uploading, application approval, and badge verification do not publish a creation.
