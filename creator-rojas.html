THE CREATOR STORY

Build the sound. Build the platform.

Cristian Rojas—known professionally as RojasOnTheBeat—is a Peruvian-American producer, songwriter, DJ, and entrepreneur whose work helped define South Florida’s internet-driven rap movement.

His story reaches beyond production: independent artist development, touring, festivals, publishing, and new bridges between American hip-hop and Latin music.
