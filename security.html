AMS WEST ENTERPRISE / CUSTOMER INFORMATION

TRUST & SECURITY.

We want fans and creators to understand how direct-to-fan music purchases work, where their downloads come from, and how to report a security concern. These are factual explanations, not independent certification badges.

Stripe-hosted checkout

Digital music payments use Stripe-hosted Checkout. The HYPHSWORLD site does not ask you to enter your card details into a custom payment form.

Private music masters

Paid music master files are held in private storage. Access to downloads is checked against a verified, active purchase license.

Limited-time downloads

Our music library issues short-lived download links to eligible signed-in buyers. Purchase and access policies are explained before checkout.

Report a security issue privately

If you notice a potential vulnerability in account access, games, creator profiles, or music checkout and downloads, contact AMS WEST privately. Include the page, the issue, and safe steps to reproduce it. Do not send us passwords, payment card numbers, secret keys, or other people's private data.

Email a Security Report

Please do not publicly disclose exploitable details or disrupt live customers while testing. We do not operate a public bug bounty program.

Your rights and our policies

Read the Privacy Notice, Site Terms, and Music Purchase & Refund Policy. For purchase support, use the Contact page.

D-U-N-S, federal registration, provider branding, or use of Stripe do not by themselves certify the security of HYPHSWORLD. We only display official outside certifications or verification marks after they have been granted and approved for use.
